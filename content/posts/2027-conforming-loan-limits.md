**The 2027 conforming loan limits have not been announced yet. As of November 19, 2026, the official one-unit limit for most of the country is still $832,750, set by the Federal Housing Finance Agency (FHFA) for 2026. FHFA usually publishes the new number in late November, and some lenders are already lending at early limits of up to $850,000.**

If you're shopping for a home this winter, or your loan amount sits near the line between conforming and jumbo, that difference can matter. Here is how the 2027 conforming loan limits will be set, what early lender limits mean, and how to plan a purchase around Thanksgiving week and the new year.

## Key Takeaways

- **The 2026 baseline is $832,750** for a one-unit home, and the high-cost ceiling is $1,249,125 (FHFA, November 25, 2025).
- **The 2027 figure is not official.** FHFA ties it to the change in its House Price Index from Q3 2025 to Q3 2026.
- **More than 50 lenders** are offering early limits of $845,000, $847,440 or $850,000, according to National Mortgage Professional.
- **FHA limits follow.** HUD typically announces new FHA limits after FHFA.
- **Rates are still above 7%.** Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.11% on November 12, 2026.

## What Is a Conforming Loan Limit?

A **conforming loan** is a conventional mortgage that meets Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines, including a maximum loan size. That maximum is the **conforming loan limit**. A loan above the limit for your county is a **jumbo loan** (also called a non-conforming loan).

The limit depends on the number of units and the county. For 2026, FHFA set:

- **$832,750** for a one-unit home in most of the U.S.
- **$1,249,125** as the one-unit ceiling in high-cost areas (150% of the baseline)
- Higher limits for two- to four-unit properties

You can look up any county on [FHFA's conforming loan limit page](https://www.fhfa.gov/data/conforming-loan-limit).

## How Will FHFA Set the 2027 Conforming Loan Limits?

The Housing and Economic Recovery Act requires FHFA to adjust the baseline each year by the change in average U.S. home prices. In its [2026 announcement](https://www.fhfa.gov/news/news-release/fhfa-announces-conforming-loan-limit-values-for-2026), FHFA said prices rose 3.26% between the third quarters of 2024 and 2025, so the baseline rose by the same percentage, an increase of $26,250.

For 2027, the math compares Q3 2025 to Q3 2026. That data has not been published yet. FHFA announced last year's limits on the same day as its third-quarter House Price Index report, and The Mortgage Reports noted on November 17, 2026 that home price growth has slowed (FHFA's monthly index showed prices up 2.4% year over year in August). A smaller price gain would mean a smaller increase in the limit than last year, but the final number is FHFA's to set.

## Why Are Some Lenders Already Using Higher Limits?

Since September 2026, several large lenders have raised their one-unit limits ahead of FHFA. [HousingWire reported](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/conforming-loan-limits-pennymac-uwm/) on September 16, 2026 that Pennymac moved to $850,000 and UWM to $847,440, joining Rocket Mortgage and CrossCountry Mortgage at $845,000. National Mortgage Professional reported this month that more than 50 lenders now use one of those three figures.

These early limits come with real caveats:

- **They are not official.** Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac cannot buy these loans until the new limit takes effect, so the lender carries that risk.
- **Rules vary.** A lender may limit early limits to certain loan types or add conditions, such as a full appraisal.
- **The final number could be lower.** Ask how a lender handles a locked loan that hasn't closed if FHFA's figure comes in below the early limit.

## Who Is Affected by the 2027 Loan Limit Change?

### Buyers whose loan lands just above $832,750

If your loan amount falls between the current limit and the new one, you may be able to use a conforming loan instead of a jumbo. Conforming loans often allow smaller down payments and more flexible credit guidelines.

### Buyers in high-cost counties

High-cost county limits are based on local median home values and capped at 150% of the baseline. They change county by county when FHFA publishes its new table.

### FHA borrowers

HUD sets FHA's floor at 65% of the conforming limit and its ceiling at 150%. For 2026, that put the FHA one-unit floor at $541,287 ([HUD Mortgagee Letter 2025-23](https://www.hud.gov/sites/default/files/hudclips/documents/2025-23hsgml.pdf)). New FHA limits typically follow FHFA's announcement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the 2027 conforming loan limit?

As of November 19, 2026, FHFA had not announced it. The official 2026 one-unit baseline is $832,750, and the high-cost ceiling is $1,249,125.

### When will FHFA announce the 2027 conforming loan limits?

FHFA usually announces new limits in late November, alongside its third-quarter House Price Index report. The 2026 limits were announced November 25, 2025. New limits typically apply to loans delivered to Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac starting January 1.

### Can I get a loan above $832,750 at conforming terms right now?

Some lenders are offering early limits of $845,000 to $850,000, according to National Mortgage Professional. These programs vary by lender and can carry extra conditions, so ask before you rely on one.

### What happens if my loan is above the conforming limit?

It is usually a jumbo loan. Jumbo loans often need a larger down payment, higher credit scores and more cash reserves, though requirements vary by lender.

### Do FHA loan limits change too?

Yes. HUD sets FHA limits as a percentage of the conforming limit and typically announces them after FHFA does.

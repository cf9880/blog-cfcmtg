**A mortgage broker compares loans from many wholesale lenders for you, while a bank can only offer its own loans. Banks may be convenient if you already bank there. Brokers give you more choices with one application. Freddie Mac research shows that comparing more rate quotes can save borrowers real money.**

## Key Takeaways

- **Banks** (retail lenders) offer only their own loan products and pricing.
- **Mortgage brokers** shop multiple **wholesale lenders** on your behalf.
- Freddie Mac found borrowers with **four or more quotes** could have saved **more than $1,200 a year** during high-rate months in late 2022.
- Compare offers using the official **Loan Estimate**, which lenders must send within three business days of your application.
- Always check a broker's license on **NMLS Consumer Access**.

## What Is a Mortgage Broker?

A **mortgage broker** is a licensed company or loan officer that works between you and the lenders. You fill out one application. The broker then compares programs and pricing from several **wholesale lenders**, which are lenders that work through brokers instead of directly with the public.

The broker helps you pick a loan, gathers your documents, and guides the file through underwriting to closing. The wholesale lender funds the loan.

## How Is a Bank (Retail Lender) Different?

A bank, credit union, or retail mortgage company lends its own money. Its loan officer can offer only that company's programs. If your situation doesn't fit its rules, you may have to start over somewhere else.

| | Mortgage broker | Bank / retail lender |
|---|---|---|
| Loan choices | Many lenders | One lender |
| Pricing | Compares wholesale pricing | Its own retail pricing |
| One application | Shopped to several lenders | Used by one lender |
| Niche loans (like DSCR) | Often available | Depends on the bank |
| Existing relationship perks | Not usually | Sometimes |

## Does Comparing Mortgage Rates Really Save Money?

Yes, and the research backs it up.

- In a [2023 study](https://www.freddiemac.com/research/insight/20230216-when-rates-are-higher-borrowers-who-shop-around-save), Freddie Mac found that from 2010 to 2021, borrowers who got quotes from two lenders lowered their rate by an average of 0.10 percentage point. In 2022, the average gain doubled to 0.20.
- During the high-rate months of October and November 2022, **two quotes** could have saved as much as **$600 a year**, and **four or more** could have saved **more than $1,200 a year**.
- An earlier [Freddie Mac analysis](https://www.freddiemac.com/fmac-resources/research/pdf/201804-Insight.pdf) found one extra quote saved about $1,500 over the life of a typical loan, and five quotes saved about $3,000.

Rates are high again today. Freddie Mac's 30-year average was 7.40% the week of October 15, 2026, compared with 6.27% a year earlier ([Freddie Mac](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms)). When rates are high, small differences between lenders matter more. A broker makes that comparison simpler.

## How Do Mortgage Brokers Get Paid?

Brokers are paid in one of two ways on each loan:

- **Lender-paid compensation:** The wholesale lender pays the broker.
- **Borrower-paid compensation:** You pay the broker directly, usually at closing.

Either way, it must be disclosed. Federal rules don't allow a broker to be paid by both you and the lender on the same loan. You'll see costs clearly on your **Loan Estimate** and, later, your **Closing Disclosure**.

## When Might a Bank Make More Sense?

A bank can be a good fit if:

- It offers a special program or relationship discount
- You want a portfolio loan the bank keeps on its own books
- You strongly prefer handling everything in one place

Even then, it's smart to get at least one more quote to compare.

## When Is a Mortgage Broker the Better Choice?

A broker often shines when you:

- Want to **compare** conventional, FHA, VA, USDA, and jumbo options in one place
- Are **self-employed** or have a less typical income
- Are an **investor** looking at a [DSCR loan](../dscr-loan-guide/)
- Are a veteran weighing a [VA home loan](../va-home-loan-guide-veterans-day/) against other programs
- Want a guide who can explain the trade-offs in plain English

## What Questions Should I Ask Any Lender?

1. Are you licensed? What is your NMLS number?
2. What loan programs can you offer me?
3. What's the rate, and how many **discount points** does it include? (Points are upfront fees paid to lower your rate.)
4. What are the total closing costs?
5. How long is the **rate lock**, and what happens if it expires?
6. How long does closing usually take?

Compare answers using the Loan Estimates side by side. Look at the rate, the points, and the total cost, not just the monthly payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between a mortgage broker and a bank?

A bank lends its own money and offers only its own loan products. A mortgage broker compares loans from multiple wholesale lenders and helps you choose one.

### Is a mortgage broker more expensive than a bank?

Not necessarily. Brokers are paid by the lender or by the borrower, and that cost is disclosed on your Loan Estimate. Because brokers can access wholesale pricing from several lenders, the total cost is often competitive.

### How much can comparing mortgage rates save?

Freddie Mac research found that during high-rate months in late 2022, borrowers with two rate quotes could have saved as much as $600 a year, and those with four or more could have saved over $1,200 a year.

### Will shopping for a mortgage hurt my credit?

Usually only a little. Credit scoring models generally treat multiple mortgage inquiries within a short window, typically 14 to 45 days depending on the model, as a single inquiry.

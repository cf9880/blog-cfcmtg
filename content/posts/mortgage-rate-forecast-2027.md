**The mortgage rate forecast for 2027 is mostly flat. Fannie Mae's November 12, 2026 forecast projects a 7.2% average 30-year fixed rate in 2027, and the Mortgage Bankers Association (MBA) expects 7.2% easing to 7.1% by late 2027. For Thanksgiving week, Freddie Mac's 30-year average dipped to 7.21%.**

Happy Thanksgiving from all of us at Coastal Funding. While the turkey's in the oven, plenty of families are also asking a money question: will mortgage rates go down next year? Here's a plain-English look at what the major forecasters expect for 2027, what rates are doing right now, and how to plan whether you're buying, refinancing, or tapping equity.

## Key Takeaways

- **Rates dipped for the holiday.** Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed averaged 7.21% as of November 25, 2026, down from 7.28% a week earlier. A year ago it was 6.23%.
- **Fannie Mae sees about 7.2% all of 2027.** Its November forecast holds the 30-year average at 7.2% in every quarter of 2027.
- **MBA sees a slow drift lower.** MBA projects 7.3% in late 2026, 7.2% for most of 2027 and 7.1% in the fourth quarter.
- **Home prices are expected to rise slightly,** not fall, in both forecasts.
- **Forecasts are not offers.** They change every month, and your rate depends on your credit, down payment, and loan type.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

*Sources: [Fannie Mae Housing Forecast, November 2026](https://www.fanniemae.com/media/58571/display); [MBA Mortgage Finance Forecast, November 17, 2026](https://www.mba.org/docs/default-source/research-and-forecasts/forecasts/2026/mortgage-finance-forecast-nov-2026.pdf). Fannie Mae notes its rate forecast is based on rates as of October 30, 2026.*

A few other views are worth knowing, with less detail behind them:

- **Goldman Sachs Research** has cited a 6.4% level for 2027 without quarterly figures, according to The Mortgage Reports.
- **The National Association of REALTORS® (NAR)** postponed its 2027 forecast to December 16, 2026, The Mortgage Reports said, citing Homes.com.

### Why do the forecasts barely move?

Mortgage rates follow longer-term bond yields, especially the 10-year Treasury, plus a spread. MBA's forecast assumes the 10-year Treasury yield averages about 5.0% in the first three quarters of 2027 and 4.9% late in the year. When forecasters expect bond yields to stay high, their mortgage rate forecasts stay high too. For more on this, see our guide to [what moves mortgage rates](/posts/what-moves-mortgage-rates/).

The gap between today and MBA's late-2027 forecast is about $26 a month on this loan. That's real money, but it's small compared with what a better credit score, a larger down payment, or seller-paid costs can do.

## Will Home Prices Fall in 2027?

The major forecasts say no, but growth slows a lot:

- **Fannie Mae** projects home prices up 0.6% from the fourth quarter of 2026 to the fourth quarter of 2027, after 2.7% in 2026.
- **MBA** projects the FHFA purchase-only house price index up 0.2% in 2027.
- **FHFA** reported home prices up 2.72% from the third quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026, which raised the 2027 conforming loan limit to $855,400 for one-unit homes in most of the country.

For more on the new limit, read our [2027 conforming loan limits guide](/posts/2027-conforming-loan-limits/), written before FHFA's November 24 announcement.

## Should You Wait for Lower Rates?

It depends on your goals. Here's a simple way to think about it:

- **Buying a home:** If the payment fits your budget today, waiting for a forecast that may not happen has a cost too. Fannie Mae projects existing-home sales of about 3.99 million in 2027, roughly flat with 2026, so quiet markets can give prepared buyers room to negotiate.
- **Refinancing:** Rates are about a point higher than a year ago, so a rate-and-term refinance makes sense for fewer people right now. Our [year-end refinance guide](/posts/should-i-refinance-before-year-end-2026/) covers when it can still help.
- **Tapping equity:** If you have a low first-mortgage rate, a HELOC (home equity line of credit) lets you borrow against your equity without replacing that rate. Coastal Funding offers HELOCs.

### Terms to know

- **30-year fixed-rate mortgage:** a loan with the same interest rate and principal-and-interest payment for 30 years.
- **Rate lock:** a lender's promise to hold a rate for a set number of days while your loan closes.
- **Forecast:** an economist's projection based on assumptions about the economy and bond markets. It is not a quote.

## What Can You Do Before Year-End?

1. **Get preapproved before the holidays wrap up.** Lenders and appraisers get busy right before New Year's.
2. **Watch the December 8-9 Federal Reserve meeting.** The Fed doesn't set mortgage rates, but its outlook can move bond yields.
3. **Compare loan types.** FHA, VA, conventional, and jumbo loans price differently. A mortgage broker can compare several lenders at once.
4. **Avoid new debt.** A holiday car loan or big card balance can change your debt-to-income ratio.

**Yes, you can use gift money for a down payment on most primary-residence mortgages, including conventional and FHA loans. The gift has to come from an acceptable donor, it cannot be a loan in disguise, and it must be documented with a signed gift letter and proof of the transfer. Plan the paperwork before the money moves, not after.**

The holidays are when a lot of families talk about money, and for some home buyers that conversation includes help with a down payment. A gift can be a big boost. It can also slow down a loan if it shows up as an unexplained deposit. This guide walks through the rules so a generous gift does not turn into an underwriting headache.

## Key Takeaways

- **Gift money for a down payment is allowed** on conventional and FHA primary-residence loans when the donor is eligible and the gift is documented.
- **A gift letter is required.** It states the amount, that no repayment is expected, and the donor's contact details and relationship to you.
- **Paper trail matters.** Lenders want to see the funds leave the donor's account and arrive in yours or at closing.
- **The 2026 IRS annual gift exclusion is $19,000 per recipient.** Larger gifts may need a gift tax return from the donor, though tax is usually not due.
- **Gifts are not allowed on Fannie Mae investment-property loans.**

## What Counts as a Down Payment Gift?

A **gift** is money (or, in some cases, home equity) given to a borrower with no expectation that it will be paid back. A **gift letter** is the signed statement from the donor that confirms it. If there is any expectation of repayment, it is a loan, and loans have to be counted in your debts.

A **gift of equity** is a related idea: a family member sells you a home for less than its value, and the difference counts toward your down payment. Fannie Mae allows gifts of equity, but they cannot be used for financial reserves.

## Who Can Give You Down Payment Money?

The answer depends on the loan program.

### Conventional loans (Fannie Mae)

According to [Fannie Mae's Selling Guide, B3-4.3-04 Personal Gifts](https://selling-guide.fanniemae.com/sel/b3-4.3-04/personal-gifts), a gift can come from:

- A **relative**: your spouse, child or other dependent, or anyone related to you by blood, marriage, adoption or legal guardianship.
- A **non-relative with a familial relationship**: a domestic partner (or their relative), a fiancé, a former relative, or someone with a long-standing familial-like or mentorship relationship.

The donor **cannot** be the builder, developer, real estate agent or any other interested party to the deal. Fannie Mae also notes that on a one-unit primary residence, **all** of the funds needed can come from a gift, even when you put less than 20% down. Two- to four-unit homes and second homes above 80% loan-to-value require a 5% contribution from your own funds first.

### FHA loans

FHA rules in HUD Handbook 4000.1, as summarized by [FHA.com](https://www.fha.com/fha_article?id=785), allow gifts from a family member, your employer or labor union, a close friend with a clearly defined and documented interest in you, a charitable organization, or a government agency or public entity with a homeownership assistance program. FHA defines gifts as contributions of cash or equity with no expectation of repayment.

VA, USDA and jumbo loans have their own gift rules. We will check the specific program before you accept the money.

## What Goes in a Gift Letter?

Fannie Mae requires the gift letter to:

1. **State the actual or maximum dollar amount** of the gift.
2. **Include the donor's statement that no repayment is expected.**
3. **List the donor's name, address, telephone number and relationship** to you.

If the gift comes from a trust or estate set up by an acceptable donor, the letter must be signed by the donor and name the trust or estate account.

## How Should the Money Be Transferred?

This is where many files get stuck. Underwriters need to follow the money. Common ways to document it include:

- The donor's bank statement showing the withdrawal **and** your statement showing the deposit.
- A copy of the donor's check plus your deposit receipt.
- A wire sent directly to the closing agent, with the closing agent's confirmation.

**Tip:** Do not deposit holiday cash gifts in small, scattered amounts right before you apply. Talk to us first so we can tell you the cleanest way to move the money for your loan type.

## Do Down Payment Gifts Trigger Gift Tax?

Usually not for the buyer. The donor is the one responsible for gift tax rules. According to the [IRS gift tax FAQ](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/frequently-asked-questions-on-gift-taxes), the annual exclusion for 2026 is **$19,000 per recipient**, and the basic lifetime exclusion is **$15,000,000** for 2026. A donor who gives more than $19,000 to one person in a year may need to file Form 709, but tax is generally owed only after the lifetime exclusion is used. This is general information, not tax advice; donors should check with a tax professional.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use gift money for a down payment?

Usually, yes. Conventional, FHA and other primary-residence loans allow gifts from acceptable donors, as long as the gift is documented with a signed gift letter and a clear paper trail showing the money moving from the donor to you or to closing.

### Who can give me down payment money for a conventional loan?

Fannie Mae allows gifts from relatives by blood, marriage, adoption or legal guardianship, and from people with a familial-type relationship such as a fiancé, domestic partner or long-standing mentor. The donor cannot be the builder, developer, real estate agent or another interested party.

### What goes in a mortgage gift letter?

Fannie Mae requires the dollar amount (or maximum amount) of the gift, a statement that no repayment is expected, and the donor's name, address, phone number and relationship to the borrower.

### Does my relative owe gift tax on a down payment gift?

For 2026 the IRS annual gift tax exclusion is $19,000 per recipient. A larger gift may require the donor to file Form 709, but tax is generally owed only after the lifetime exclusion is used. Ask a tax professional about your situation.

### Can I use gift money on an investment property?

Not on a Fannie Mae conventional investment-property loan; Fannie Mae's guide says gifts are not allowed on investment properties. Gift funds are mainly for primary residences and, with limits, second homes.

**The Federal Reserve meets October 27–28, 2026, and most forecasters expect it to hold its benchmark rate at 3.75%–4.00% on Wednesday. That decision does not set mortgage rates directly. The 30-year fixed averaged 7.33% on October 26, and what the Fed signals about December may matter more than this week's vote.**

If you plan to buy, refinance, or tap equity before the end of the year, here is what to watch this week and what you can do now, without trying to guess the headlines.

## Key Takeaways

- **The decision comes Wednesday, October 28.** Most forecasters expect a hold after September's quarter-point hike, the first since 2023.
- **Markets are looking at December.** In a Reuters poll, all 100 economists expect a December hike, and most expect a hold this week.
- **Mortgage rates are already high:** 7.33% for a 30-year fixed on October 26 (Mortgage News Daily), up 1.14 points from a year ago.
- **Mortgage rates track bond yields, not the Fed's rate.** The Fed's tone and inflation data move those yields.
- **Plan around your own timeline.** Get preapproved, know your lock options, and avoid big credit changes before closing.

## When Is the Fed Meeting and What Is Expected?

The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meets **Tuesday and Wednesday, October 27–28, 2026**. The decision and Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference follow on Wednesday afternoon. In September, the Fed raised its target range by a quarter point to **3.75%–4.00%**, its first increase since 2023, because inflation has been running hot. Annual CPI inflation was **3.7%**, according to CNBC's October 26 preview. See the Fed's [official meeting calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm).

Most forecasters expect a pause this week:

| Signal | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Reuters poll (October 2026) | 94 of 100 economists expect a hold this week; all 100 expect a December hike |
| CME FedWatch (per CNBC) | Under 10% odds of a hike this week; about 75% odds of a hike by year-end |
| Morningstar | Pause odds near 90% |

A hold this week is not the same as "rates are going down." The statement, the press conference, and the next inflation reports will shape where mortgage rates go into the holidays.

## Does the Fed Set Mortgage Rates?

No. The Fed sets a short-term, overnight rate. Most 30-year mortgage rates follow longer-term bond yields, especially the 10-year Treasury, plus a spread that lenders and investors add for risk. If the Fed sounds more worried about inflation, long-term yields can rise even when the Fed does nothing. If it sounds calmer, yields can ease. We cover this in more detail in [what moves mortgage rates](/posts/what-moves-mortgage-rates/).

The Fed's rate does affect some loans more directly. **HELOCs, many credit cards, and some adjustable-rate products** are usually tied to the prime rate, which tends to move with the Fed's target.

## 5 Moves to Make Before Year-End

### 1. Get fully preapproved now

A written preapproval tells you your real price range at today's rates. It also makes your offer stronger with sellers who want a smooth holiday closing. See our [winter and holiday home buying guide](/posts/winter-holiday-home-buying-guide/) for timelines.

### 2. Know your lock options before you sign a contract

Ask how long a lock lasts (often 30, 45, or 60 days), what an extension costs, and whether a float-down option is available if rates drop after you lock. If you're closing in December, a lock that covers holiday delays can be worth more than a slightly lower rate on a short lock.

### 3. Compare a buydown with a lower price

When sellers are motivated, a seller credit used for a temporary or permanent rate buydown can lower your payment more than a small price cut. Ask your loan officer to compare both options with real numbers.

### 4. Shop more than one lender

Rates and fees vary by lender on the same day. As a broker, we compare options from multiple wholesale lenders in one application. Learn the difference in [mortgage broker vs. bank](/posts/mortgage-broker-vs-bank/).

### 5. Keep your credit steady until closing

Don't open new cards, finance furniture, or move large sums without a paper trail before closing. Late-year spending is one of the [scary mortgage myths](/posts/scary-mortgage-myths-halloween-2026/) that's actually true: it can delay or derail an approval.

## Should Homeowners Refinance or Wait?

With rates above 7%, most owners who locked in lower rates in past years won't benefit from a standard rate-and-term refinance today. If you need cash for a project, compare a home equity line of credit or a home equity loan with a cash-out refinance. Keep in mind that HELOC rates usually rise when the Fed raises rates. If you bought within the past year at a rate near today's highs, ask us to set a target rate and watch it for you.

### When is the next Fed meeting in 2026?

The FOMC meets October 27–28, 2026, and the decision comes Wednesday afternoon. The Fed meets again in December, and a Reuters poll found all 100 economists surveyed expect a hike at that meeting.

### Will the Fed raise rates in October 2026?

### Do mortgage rates go up when the Fed raises rates?

Not always. Mortgage rates follow longer-term bond yields. They can move before, after, or opposite to a Fed decision, depending on inflation data and the Fed's outlook.
